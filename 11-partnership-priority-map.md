# Collin County Partnership Priority Map

## Tier A — Safety and crisis coordination
Priority outreach:
- Collin County Sheriff's Office Victim Assistance
- Collin County District Attorney Victim Assistance
- Hope's Door New Beginning Center
- The Turning Point
- Children's Advocacy Center of Collin County
- Texas Council on Family Violence

Collin County's Victim Assistance Program explicitly provides safety planning, counseling/community referrals, crisis intervention and protective-order assistance, and its local-resource list includes Hope's Door, The Turning Point and The Samaritan Inn. ## Partnership proposition
Feel at Home Connect is not asking organizations to replace their existing intake systems. It gives community members a clearer digital path to the organization's existing services while giving providers a structured way to keep public information current.

## First partner meeting agenda
1. What services do you provide?
2. Who is eligible?
3. What is the intake process?
4. What information changes most often?
5. What information should never be publicly displayed?
6. How should users be directed during an emergency?
7. Who should own profile updates?
8. How frequently can information be reverified?
