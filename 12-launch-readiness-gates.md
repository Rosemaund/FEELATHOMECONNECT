# Launch Readiness Gates

The app should not move from pilot to broad public launch until these gates are documented.

## Gate 1 — Product
- Core journey works end to end
- Search is understandable
- Resource details are actionable
- Start Over Plan works
- Saved resources work

## Gate 2 — Data
- Initial target of 100+ verified resources
- Resource ownership identified
- Verification dates recorded
- High-risk/stale records reviewed
- User reporting workflow operational

## Gate 3 — Safety
- Domestic-violence advocate review completed
- Confidential-location handling reviewed
- Quick Exit behavior reviewed
- Emergency language reviewed
- Safety Center tested

## Gate 4 — Privacy/security
- Data-minimization review
- Authentication/security review
- Access-control review
- Encryption confirmed
- Administrative audit trail confirmed
- Privacy policy and consent language reviewed by qualified counsel

## Gate 5 — Accessibility
- Screen-reader review
- color-contrast review
- text scaling
- touch target review
- plain-language review
- multilingual strategy defined

## Gate 6 — Pilot
- Collin County partner testing
- community-user testing
- provider testing
- issue triage
- measurable baseline metrics

## Gate 7 — Operations
- Resource verification owner assigned
- Partner onboarding process documented
- Support/contact process established
- Incident escalation process established
- Release/rollback process established
